How matching works

I use Open Library subjects to find books related to the ones you already enjoy. Choose up to three subjects, and Bookmatch ranks the results by how many of those subjects they share.

You can also filter for a different author or publication era. Open Library's subject data can be incomplete, so the matching subjects are shown with each recommendation.
